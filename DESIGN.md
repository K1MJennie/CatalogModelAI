# Moezza design direction

Source: user brief and three app screenshots. Moezza is both the startup and its first app. The user corrected the name to two z characters.

Reading: startup profile for Android users, warm editorial layout and a friendly product preview. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 1.

- Colors: screenshot-derived mint for the introduction, blue for technology, blush for the startup story, and forest green for readable text and primary actions.
- Layout: open product selection rows, a handset preview, an explanatory device boundary, a large startup statement, a short company profile, and a contact form correspond to distinct reader needs.
- Typography: Trebuchet MS gives headings a friendly character; system sans-serif body text reads comfortably and needs no external font service. The italic serif word in the mission emphasizes the human intention.
- Spacing: larger gaps separate company narrative from product details; smaller gaps keep conversation messages, profile labels, and form labels together.
- Motif: speech-bubble corners connect the brand mark, product preview, and conversation examples.
- Preview: the panda was removed at the owner's request. The handset now shows a simpler chat sequence and is labelled as a concept, not an actual screenshot.
- Radius: 5-7 px controls, 13 px messages, and a rounded handset convey different kinds of objects instead of rounding every section.
- Shadow: one substantial shadow gives the handset depth. The expanded mobile menu uses a small elevation shadow.
- Light theme: fixed pale styling follows the explicit reference palette. There is no theme toggle.
- Motion: 160 ms control hover feedback, optional anchor scrolling, no looping animation; reduced-motion preference disables transitions and smooth scrolling.
- Arrow icons identify navigation direction. Device, chat, and chip icons explain their adjacent content. No external icon/font dependency.
- Demo: three author-written examples, explicitly marked as curated rather than live inference. No fabricated metrics or endorsements.
- Contact: the user permitted dummy data and requested the `@moezza.ai.org` domain. The About page labels `halo@moezza.ai.org` and `+62 800 0000 0000` as inactive examples. They have no direct action. The form prepares a local draft and clearly states that it does not send messages.

Manual owner review: confirm company voice, product compatibility, visual hierarchy on a real phone, and eventual real contact channels. Test keyboard navigation, topic changes, draft preview, and download. This website does not establish device inference performance.
